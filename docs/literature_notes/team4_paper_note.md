# Literature Note — Team 4 Paper

## Paper

Ferreira et al., A comparative review of deep and spiking neural networks for edge AI neuromorphic circuits, Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2025.

## Relevance

The paper compares DNN and SNN approaches for edge-AI neuromorphic circuits, emphasizing hardware-aware metrics such as energy efficiency, area efficiency, neuron models, and digital versus analog implementations.

## Key Takeaway

DNNs remain the practical baseline because of mature training tools and strong accuracy. SNNs are promising for sparse, event-driven, temporal sensing under strict power constraints.

## Connection to This Project

The project starts with a minimal spike-based edge-inference demonstration and may later extend toward sensing features, PYNQ, and Vitis HLS.
