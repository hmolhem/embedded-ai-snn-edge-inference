\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Actuation & Physical execution of a command through a motor, brake, steering mechanism, valve, relay, or other actuator. \\
Asynchronous processing & Processing triggered or localized by events rather than repeated full-batch or full-frame execution. \\
Attention & Learned input-dependent weighting of tokens, features, channels, locations, time steps, or modalities. \\
Calibration & Estimation of sensor-internal parameters and spatial transformations among sensors. \\
Closed loop & A system in which actions change the physical world and new measurements feed back into future decisions. \\
Compute-in-memory & Computing near or within memory to reduce data movement, commonly for matrix operations. \\
Context & Environmental or system condition that changes which sensing or model configuration is appropriate. \\
Data association & Determining which measurements correspond to the same physical object or landmark. \\
Decision-level fusion & Combination of independent decisions, scores, hypotheses, or tracks. \\
Event camera & Sensor that reports pixel-level brightness changes as asynchronous events. \\
Event-driven & A data or execution model in which activity occurs when events are present. \\
Extrinsic calibration & Rotation and translation relating one sensor coordinate frame to another. \\
Feature & Engineered or learned representation that preserves task-relevant information while reducing raw-data complexity. \\
Feature-level fusion & Combination of intermediate features produced by modality-specific encoders. \\
Fusion & Combination of multiple information sources to improve an estimate or decision. \\
GNN & Graph neural network; a model that updates nodes through relationships and message passing. \\
Graceful degradation & Controlled reduction of capability when sensors or computational resources are lost. \\
IMU & Inertial measurement unit containing accelerometers and gyroscopes, and sometimes magnetometers. \\
Intrinsic calibration & Internal sensor parameters such as camera focal length and lens distortion. \\
Latency & Time from an input event or measurement to the required output. \\
LiDAR & Light Detection and Ranging; optical range sensing commonly producing a 3-D point cloud. \\
Loose coupling & Fusion of subsystem-level state estimates rather than direct joint use of their measurements. \\
Measurement & A sensor observation with a time, coordinate frame, unit, and uncertainty. \\
Modality & A distinct type of information defined by sensing physics and data structure, such as RGB, depth, event, radar, or inertial data. \\
Multimodal system & A system that uses more than one information modality. \\
Near-sensor computing & Computation placed at or close to the sensor to filter, compress, or interpret data before transfer. \\
Neuromorphic computing & Brain-inspired computing with neuron/synapse structures, local state, and often event-driven communication. \\
NPU/DPU & Specialized processor for neural-network operations. \\
Observability & The extent to which hidden state variables can be inferred from available measurements. \\
Odometry & Estimation of incremental platform motion over time. \\
Open loop & Evaluation or operation without feedback from the output to future system input. \\
Perception & Interpretation of sensor data into objects, free space, scene structure, or semantic information. \\
Point cloud & Collection of three-dimensional points with optional intensity, time, and channel attributes. \\
Representation & Structured form in which data are presented to an algorithm. \\
Robustness & Ability to maintain acceptable behavior under noise, environmental change, and system degradation. \\
Runtime reconfiguration & Changing active sensors, model branches, precision, or hardware configuration during operation. \\
Sensor & Physical device that converts a physical phenomenon into a measurable signal. \\
Sensor fusion & Combination of multisensor information with alignment, uncertainty, and consistency management. \\
SLAM & Simultaneous Localization and Mapping. \\
SNN & Spiking neural network using spike events and time-dependent neuron states. \\
State & Set of hidden physical variables sufficient to describe and predict system behavior. \\
State estimation & Inference of system state from dynamics, controls, measurements, and uncertainty. \\
Synchronization & Alignment of measurement timestamps to a common time reference. \\
Tight coupling & Joint use of measurements or residuals from multiple sensors in a shared estimator. \\
Throughput & Number of samples or decisions processed per unit time. \\
Uncertainty & Quantified lack of certainty caused by measurement variation, ambiguity, or model knowledge. \\
