\section{Project Timeline}

The Phase 2 work will be completed in a staged sequence. The proposed timeline is shown in Table~\ref{tab:phase2_timeline}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Proposed Phase 2 Timeline}
\label{tab:phase2_timeline}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.22\textwidth} p{0.68\textwidth}}
\toprule
Stage & Planned Work \\
\midrule
Stage 1 & Finalize the Phase 2 proposal and confirm the LaTeX template structure. \\
Stage 2 & Reproduce the Phase 1-style baseline inside the Phase 2 working directory. \\
Stage 3 & Extend the sensing dataset and add robustness-oriented test cases. \\
Stage 4 & Run continuous-baseline and LIF-inspired inference experiments. \\
Stage 5 & Generate figures, tables, and result summaries for the final report. \\
Stage 6 & Write and polish the Phase 2 final technical report. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

This staged schedule is intended to reduce technical risk. The project first confirms that the existing pipeline still works, then adds difficulty gradually, and finally reports the results in a controlled and reproducible manner.
